AI Architecture & Guide

Model Pipeline Overview

The recognition engine uses a multi-stage deep learning pipeline. Frames from your webcam are processed by MediaPipe Holistic to extract 3D spatial coordinate tensors across hands, pose, and face.

Vision Engine MediaPipe Holistic
3D Keypoints 75 Landmarks / Frame
Sequence Model Bidirectional LSTM
Temporal Window 30 Frames
Speech Output Sinhala TTS Cache
Privacy Zero Frame Storage

Tips for Optimal Accuracy

1. Lighting: Ensure your hands and face are evenly lit without strong backlighting.

2. Posture: Position yourself ~0.7 to 1.2 meters from the camera so both hands are clearly within the frame.

3. Timing: Perform signs smoothly and hold the final pose briefly for the temporal window buffer to reach 100%.
