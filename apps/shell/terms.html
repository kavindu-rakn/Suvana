An academic demo

Suvana is a research project for Sri Lankan Sign Language communication and learning. This deployment is non-commercial and academic. There is no paid offering.

Use it as practice

A Learn score measures a match with one saved sign. It does not prove that a sign is correct or that someone understands it. Recognition, captions, emotion estimates and assistant answers can be wrong.

Use a qualified interpreter when clear communication matters. Do not rely on this demo for emergency, medical or legal decisions. Phone alerts and SOS features depend on permissions, the device and available services.

Respect people and content

Only record or contribute someone else's voice or images with their permission. Do not upload secrets or material you have no right to share.

Kaggle landmark references are marked CC0. Yohan landmark references use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial use, credit and share-alike terms apply. These labels do not grant rights to corpus video or anyone's likeness. No corpus video is cleared for this demo.

Your account and device

Keep your password private. Learn progress lives in the browser you use and may be lost when its data is cleared. Features may be unavailable while a service is offline.
