A future plan, with nothing for sale

This page describes an idea for the future. There are no prices, subscriptions or payments here.

The open-core idea

The idea is to keep a useful core open, with possible services for organisations later. What those services include, their licences and their costs still need a separate decision. This page is not an offer to sell anything.

Content comes first

This academic demo includes Kaggle CC0 and Yohan CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 landmark files. Yohan content is for non-commercial use. You must give credit and follow the share-alike rules.

A future commercial version would need content we have permission to use and a separate review of those rights. An option using only CC0 content is planned. It is not a paid product or a promise that a commercial version is ready.

Hosting has rules too

Vercel's Hobby plan is only for personal, non-commercial use. Any commercial plan for Suvana must follow the hosting terms too. Calling this a roadmap does not, by itself, mean those terms are met.

Read Vercel's Hobby plan terms.

Still a research demo

Sign content, device tests and whether services are available need more review before any launch. We make no claims here about better learning or accurate sign recognition.
