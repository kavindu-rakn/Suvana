Roadmap: no paid offering

This page describes an idea for the future. There are no prices, subscriptions or payments here.

The open-core idea

The proposal is to keep a useful core open, with possible services for organisations later. The scope, licence and costs of any future service still need a separate decision. Nothing on this page is an offer to sell.

Content comes first

The current academic edition includes Kaggle CC0 and Yohan CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 landmark references. Yohan content is for non-commercial use with credit and share-alike terms.

A future commercial edition would need permitted content and a separate rights review. A CC0-only content option is planned; it is not a paid product or a promise that a commercial edition is ready.

Hosting has limits too

Vercel's Hobby plan is restricted to personal, non-commercial use. Any commercial plan for Suvana must also meet the hosting terms. Calling a page a roadmap does not by itself establish compliance.

Read Vercel's Hobby plan terms.

Still a research demo

Sign content, device checks and service availability need further review before any launch. This roadmap makes no claim about learning gains or recognition accuracy.
