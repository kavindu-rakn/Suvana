Sound awareness · Companion app

Alerts

The one part of Suvana that is not a web page. Alerts runs on the phone in a Deaf user's pocket, listening for the sounds they cannot hear — and it can raise an SOS with their location when something goes wrong.

Download Android APK Android only · 267 MB

Two modes, one microphone

Awareness, and understanding.

The two halves serve the same person in completely different situations, so the app hands the microphone cleanly between them rather than running both at once.

Listen

Environmental sound awareness

An on-device classifier watches for sirens, alarms, horns, doorbells and knocking, and raises a high-contrast visual alert with a distinct haptic signature per sound — so the phone can be understood in a pocket, without looking at it.

  • Runs on the device with ONNX Runtime — no audio is uploaded
  • Escalating alerts, so a smoke alarm does not feel like a doorbell
  • Keeps working with the screen off via a background service
Transcribe

Live speech captions

Turns the conversation happening in front of the user into readable text on the phone, so a hearing person who does not sign can still be understood in a shop, a clinic or a classroom.

  • Offline captions depend on your device and installed language pack
  • Sized for reading at arm's length, not for a transcript archive
  • Hands the microphone back to Listen the moment it is closed
SOS

Safety circle

A Safety Circle of trusted contacts, and a one-touch emergency beacon that sends them an SMS with the user's current GPS location.

  • SMS, so it works without the recipient installing anything
  • Location attached automatically
  • History of what was detected and what was sent

Installing

Install Alerts on Android.

Download the APK and install it on your phone. No computer is needed. Sound awareness works offline after installation. Live captions may need internet unless on-device recognition is enabled with an offline language pack.

  1. Download Suvana Alerts

    Open this page on your Android phone and download the APK. Version 1.0.0 · 267 MB · Android only.

    Download Android APK

  2. Install on your phone

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

  3. Open Alerts and start listening

    Allow microphone access for sound awareness. The app listens on your phone and works without a computer or internet connection.
