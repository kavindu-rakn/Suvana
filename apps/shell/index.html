Architecture & AI Pipeline

Bridging Vision, Sound & Avatars

Suvana coordinates four specialized deep learning and spatial computing systems to power full-duplex communication in Sri Lankan Sign Language.

01

Spatial Computer Vision

Extracts 75 3D keypoints across hands, face contours, and pose landmarks in real time using MediaPipe Holistic at 60 FPS.

On-Device Edge
02

Temporal Sequence AI

Processes continuous 30-frame temporal gesture windows with deep LSTM and Transformer sequence models to decode dynamic signs.

Sequence Classifier
03

Sinhala TTS & 3D Avatar

Instantly speaks Sinhala translations with warmed audio cache, and synthesizes 3D signing avatars with vocal emotion detection.

Speech & Graphics
04

Acoustic Awareness

Mobile edge classifier detects environmental hazard sounds like sirens, alarms, and horns, sending haptic and flash alerts.

SoundGuard Ecosystem

How Sign Recognition Works

From raw camera frames to synthesized Sinhala speech: our pipeline isolates signer kinematics, normalizes 3D spatial coordinate tensors, classifies sequence patterns, and outputs speech synthesis with zero server storage.
