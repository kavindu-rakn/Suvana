### Resulting behavior

- Typed `Hint` objects carry kind, learner hand or duration ratio as appropriate; `ScoreResult.typedHints` is populated on every scoring path, while legacy string `hints` preserves existing consumers and older fixture types.
- Close match appears only at score >=85, with a neutral reference-comparison hint below it when nothing else applies.
- Timing compares the span from first to last tracked hand frame with reference.durationMs, excluding leading/trailing no-hand frames. The [0.67, 1.5] interval is inclusive and explicitly untuned; ratios round to one decimal. Invalid/empty durations produce no timing claim.
- Per-hand weighted shape/trajectory contributions are measured along the **existing winning DTW path**. For a path hint, aggregate trajectory must exceed aggregate shape, with reference-frame weighting as used for score. Nothing changes alignment, score weighting, anchors or score output.
- At most 2 hints: structural missing-hand/framing first, then the dominant path contribution, then timing. No unvalidated per-finger corrections. Missing-hand names are flipped into the learner's anatomical side when the selected comparison was mirrored; an integrated two-handed regression covers this.
