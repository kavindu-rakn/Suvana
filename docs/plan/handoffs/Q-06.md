## Result (kvn's words, summarised)

- Everything worked, except one failure:
- **Browser Back to the homepage shows a white screen.** It happened when going
  back to the homepage from Learn, Communicate, the login and sign-up pages,
  Recognize and Alerts.

kvn gave no per-device breakdown beyond this; no other failure was reported.

## Cause and fix

- Cause: the homepage's link handler (`apps/shell/src/main.ts`, section 10)
  slides its full-screen preloader up to cover the page, then navigates. On
  Back, the browser restores the homepage from its back/forward cache exactly
  as it was left, still covered by the preloader, so the screen is blank.
- Fix: `agent/claude/shell-back-button` (110de73) hides the preloader, stops
  its tween and restores scrolling when `pageshow` reports a restored page
  (`event.persisted`). Merged into `integration/2026-10-18f`.
- Verified locally by firing a restored `pageshow` on a covered homepage: the
  preloader is hidden. A real Back press could not be replayed in the agent's
  browser, so kvn re-checks Back on a phone after the deploy.
- Only the homepage has this exit animation; Alerts and the module pages do
  not, so no other page needs the fix.
