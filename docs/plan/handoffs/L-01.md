### Decision taken in chat (kvn, 13 Oct, during this task): for DECISIONS.md
**Per-finger states are hidden for now.** The result screen shows no per-finger state and no hand diagram. It keeps the measured overall score, the per-hand scores of a two-handed sign, and the "Focus on" worst-finger chips. Per-finger states come back with L-17 once a method passes a same-sign vs different-sign check. This amends DN-17 / D-A12 (which chose Correct/Almost/Wrong): the wording stays, display is deferred.

Why (measured with the real scorer on the calibration corpus in `C:\Users\User\Documents\Suvana\apps\learn\calibration`: 557 takes, 33 signs, one fluent signer; worst-joint deviation per finger, hand-size units; reproduced by `fingerStates.calibration.test.ts`):
- Same sign (take vs take, correct renditions): p10 0.305, p50 0.790, p75 1.159, p90 1.691. 99.7% of fingers exceed 0.1, and 98.9% do even in pairs scoring 85 or more.
- Different sign (neighbouring gloss): p10 0.710, p50 1.371, p75 1.848, p90 2.469.
- Variant A (spec: Wrong > 0.1, Almost > 0.05): every finger reads Wrong on a correct CHOOSE take scoring 79 (screenshot `rejected-A-*`).
- Variant B (Correct up to 1.159 = p75, Almost up to 1.691 = p90): the correct take looks right, but a wrong sign (FEBRUARY take scored against ADINAWA, 69) shows every finger Correct (`rejected-B-*-wrongsign`).
